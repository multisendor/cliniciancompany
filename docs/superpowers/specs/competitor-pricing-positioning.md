## 2. Pricing landscape map

### Three tiers

**Budget tier (< $30 single bottle)**
- Doctor's Best 2,000 FU 90-cap — $14.99-22
- Nutricost / Double Wood / generic Amazon private-label — $18-25
- Source Naturals 30-ct entry SKU — borderline

These compete on raw $/cap. They are not in the same buying decision as TCC. They function as the floor that the educated price-comparer uses to anchor "is your premium worth it?"

**Mid tier ($30-60 single bottle)**
- Source Naturals NSK-SD 60-ct — $35.98
- Life Extension Nattokinase 90-cap — $27.75 (overlaps budget)
- Life Extension VenoFlow — $39.75
- ARG Nattokinase 60sg — $48.99
- Vital Nutrients ~$45-55

This is the practitioner-curious / educated-consumer band. They have heard of NSK-SD, want a real brand, and won't pay funnel-tier margin. **TCC's $39 Pure entry SKU lives here.**

- Designs for Health Natto-Serrazime — $73.99

- Japanese imports premium — $60-90+
- TCC Pure 90-cap @ $89 — **lands here**
- TCC Complete 90-cap @ $79 — borderline mid/premium

## 3. TCC positioning recommendation

### TCC Pure ladder: $39 / $89 / $229 / $409

**$39 entry SKU:** Reads as a 30-cap trial / 1-month-light tier. Mid-tier placement. Fair against Source Naturals 60ct at $35.98 if TCC has fewer servings — but only if the FU per serving is ≥2,000 FU. **Recommendation:** Position as 30-serving trial bottle at 4,000 FU. Frame as "try the clinical dose for a month," not as a budget play. **Hold the price.**

**$89 main SKU (90-cap @ 4,000 FU):** This is TCC's hero. Lands directly opposite TWC at the same $89.99 with 3x the servings at the same per-serving FU. **This is the strongest move in the ladder. Hold.**

**$229 — 3-bottle / 90-day:** $76.33/bottle effective. Implies ~14% multi-bottle discount. **Recommendation:** Verify against Resilia's "Buy 2 get 1" pattern — that effectively makes the 3-pack 33% off, far steeper. Consider a 3-pack at $209 (~$69.66/bottle, 22% off) to match the funnel-tier expectation more aggressively. Current $229 is a margin-protection play; safe but leaves conversion lift on the table.

**$409 — 6-bottle / 6-month:** $68.16/bottle, ~23% off single. **Recommendation:** This is well-priced for the deepest-loyalty buyer. Consider holding $409 but pairing it with an annual subscribe-and-save lock at ~$59/bottle to create a "lifer" tier. A single subscriber at $59/mo for 12 = $708 LTV; 6-pack one-time = $409 LTV. The subscribe lock is the better LTV play.

### TCC Complete ladder: $79 / $209 / $379

Complete = nattokinase + the McCullough-protocol companions (bromelain, curcumin, etc.). Direct comp is TWC Ultimate Spike Detox at $89.99 for 30 servings.

**$79 single — TCC Complete:** Beats TWC by $10 single-bottle, with what should be more servings or equal serving count. Strong if TCC delivers ≥30 servings with full-protocol dosing. **Hold $79 if servings = 30+. If servings drop to 30 with 2 caps/day = 15 days, raise to $89 to match TWC and compete on COA/trust rather than price.**

**$209 — 3-pack:** $69.66/bottle, ~12% off. **Recommendation:** Match Resilia funnel pattern — push to $199 (3-pack at $66.33/bottle, ~16% off) to make the 3-pack the obvious choice. The $10 give-up on this rung returns multiples in average-order-value lift.

**$379 — 6-pack:** $63.16/bottle, ~20% off. **Hold** — this rung is correctly priced for the McCullough-loyalist buyer who pre-ordered TWC bundles.

## 4. The 3 positioning windows TCC can credibly own

### Window 1: Honest dosing transparency (cost-per-FU disclosed openly)

**No competitor on this list publishes cost-per-FU on the PDP.** TWC explicitly hides it (their 4,000-vs-8,000 framing is engineered to obscure per-serving math). Doctor's Best lets the buyer do the math. Pure Encap and ARG just price it and walk away.

**TCC move:** Print "$/FU: $0.000247" or, more readably, "**$0.99 per clinical 4,000-FU daily dose**" directly on the PDP and the comparison page. Tag a "Why we publish this" footnote that walks through the formula. This is the single most defensible move in the doc — it cannot be copied by funnel-tier brands without dismantling their pricing model.

### Window 2: Batch COA published every batch

Out of 12 brands surveyed, **none publish a per-batch COA on a public URL.** Pure Encap will mail a COA on request; ARG ditto; TWC claims GMP without specificity; Thorne publishes NSF certification but not per-batch COA on nattokinase (no SKU exists). TCC publishing a per-batch COA — viewable by lot number on the bottle, scanned via QR — is a category-empty move.

**TCC move:** QR code on every bottle → public batch COA page showing FU verification, heavy-metal panel, microbial panel, K2 absence verification, soy/chickpea source disclosure. Cite the testing lab by name. Date each batch.

### Window 3: "Practitioner-grade single ingredient" with K2 sold separately

The premium-tier playbook is "single-ingredient discipline" (Pure Encap, ARG, Vital Nutrients). The funnel-tier playbook is "stack everything in" (TWC). TCC can split the difference cleanly:

- **Pure SKU** = single-ingredient discipline at clinical 4,000 FU dose, K2 removed, transparent.
- **K2 sold separately** as a $19-29 companion SKU, so the buyer who wants the bone-health benefit chooses to add it instead of getting it forced.
- **Complete SKU** = full McCullough-protocol stack for the buyer who wants one-bottle convenience.

This gives TCC a ladder that respects both buyer mental models: the practitioner-curious purist *and* the McCullough-protocol full-stack buyer. No competitor offers both rails cleanly.

## 5. Pricing risks / threats to monitor

### Risk 1: TWC discount cycles

Confirmed observed cycles (from current scan):
- **Black Friday 2024/2025:** 25% off all supplements + 20% off medical kits/memberships (effective TWC Ultimate price: $67.49)
- **Standing 15% codes:** TV15, FOX15, GET15, members 15% — effectively a permanent ~$76 floor for any motivated buyer
- **Bundle "$40 off" Long-COVID stack** seasonal
- **Affiliate funnels (Matt Kim, Fox News landing pages)** carry 15% baked in

**TCC implication:** TWC's *real* price for a motivated buyer is closer to $76, not $89.99. TCC at $89 single-bottle competes against TWC's $76 effective. TCC's *3x-the-servings* arithmetic still wins decisively at $89 vs. $76, but the marketing copy must compare TCC single-bottle to TWC *post-code* price, not list. Otherwise TCC looks more expensive than it is.

**Action:** Build TCC's first-bottle price-anchor against the realistic competitive price ($76, not $89.99), and let the *value-per-day* arithmetic ($0.99 vs. $2.53/day) carry the narrative.

### Risk 2: Amazon private-label entries

Amazon has direct private-label nattokinase moves underway (Double Wood at 4,000 FU, Solaray, Nutricost at 2,000 FU at $18-25, plus generic 5,000-FU SKUs at $30-40). These will commodify the high-FU claim within 12-18 months. **TCC cannot win on the FU-per-cap arms race long-term.** Amazon's private-label margin tolerance is below what TCC's brand-direct model can match.

**Action:** Do not anchor positioning on "highest FU" — anchor on **transparency, per-batch COA, source disclosure**. These are positions Amazon private-label cannot credibly occupy because they require a verifiable supply chain and a published-page strategy that doesn't fit Amazon's listing model.

### Risk 3: Subscribe-and-save floor race

If a competitor (likely Doctor's Best or a Life Extension successor SKU) drops a 4,000-FU subscribe-floor at $39-49/month, TCC's $89 single price gets visibly squeezed. The floor for clinical-dose 4,000 FU subscribe is currently un-set in the category — *it does not exist yet*.

**Action:** TCC should *establish the subscribe floor first* at $59/mo for Pure and ~$65/mo for Complete. Owning the floor is more defensible than chasing one. Lock 12-month subscribers in at $59 before a competitor sets it at $49 and TCC has to follow them down.

### Risk 4: McCullough endorsement leverage shift

TWC's pricing power is largely McCullough's brand equity. If McCullough endorses or licenses to a second brand (or his Foundation launches its own SKU at lower price), TWC's $89.99 cracks. Several "McCullough's Original" SKUs already exist on Amazon at $30-50 — the brand-equity moat is weaker than it looks.

**Action:** TCC should not bet on competing for McCullough's endorsement. Instead, position as **"the brand for the McCullough protocol that doesn't need McCullough's name on the bottle"** — a transparency-first brand that earns trust through COA and dosing math, not personality endorsement. This is durable; an endorsement is rented.

### Risk 5: NSK-SD raw-material price pass-through

Japan Bio Science Laboratories (NSK-SD® licensor) periodically raises raw-material price; non-NSK-SD nattokinase (chickpea-derived, fungal-derived) is cheaper but commands less trust. If JBSL raises prices, premium-tier brands raise to $95-110 and TCC's $89 looks better. If JBSL does *not* raise and a chickpea-derived 4,000-FU enters at $39, the bottom drops out of the mid-tier.

**Action:** TCC should disclose its strain (NSK-SD or specific Bacillus subtilis strain) on the bottle. If TCC sources NSK-SD, this is a category trust signal and a $20 price-defense argument. If TCC sources a non-NSK-SD strain to manage cost, the per-batch COA strategy must compensate.

## 6. Final pricing recommendation summary

1. **Hold Pure $89 / 90-cap @ 4,000 FU.** This is the strongest single SKU price in the ladder vs. the competitive set. It beats TWC 3x on servings at parity single-bottle price.

2. **Test Pure $209 (3-pack) instead of $229.** Match Resilia/funnel-tier multi-bottle expectations. Conversion lift will exceed margin give-up.

3. **Hold Pure $409 6-pack but layer a $59/mo subscribe lock above it.** LTV play beats one-shot.

4. **Verify Complete $79 has ≥30 servings before launching.** If not, raise to $89 and compete on COA, not price.

5. **Test Complete $199 (3-pack) instead of $209.** Same logic as Pure.

6. **Hold Complete $379 6-pack.**

7. **Print $/FU and $/clinical-dose-day on every PDP and comparison page.** This is TCC's one unique pricing-language asset.

8. **Publish per-batch COA via QR code on every bottle.** Category-empty move; defensible for 12-18 months minimum.

9. **Sell K2 separately at $19-29.** Don't bundle by default. Let the practitioner-curious buyer self-select.

10. **Anchor competitive narrative against TWC's *post-code* effective price ($76), not list ($89.99).** Otherwise TCC looks more expensive than it is.
