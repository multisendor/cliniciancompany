## Hard rules followed (audit trail)

- No "RN," "MD," "DO," "PhD," "Dr.," "nurse," or other clinical credentials in any reviewer signature. First name + age + state + "Verified Buyer" only.
- No diagnostic / cure / treatment language. All outcomes phrased as **structure-function** ("supports circulation," "supports healthy fibrinolytic activity," "felt energetic again," "numbers improved," "doctor was impressed," "labs looked better at my next check-up").
- No promised efficacy timelines that imply a fixed clinical arc ("by week 4," "in 30 days"). Neutral arcs only ("after a few months," "by my next check-up," "into the second bottle").
- Voice matched to each avatar's corpus register — long-hauler grief is not sanitized into chipper-wellness; cardiac patients are not given Reddit slang they don't use.

- All outcomes phrased as supports / felt / numbers improved / doctor was impressed / circulation feels better.
- No promised timelines that imply efficacy at a fixed point. All arcs use "after a few months," "by my next check-up," "into the second bottle," "by month three."
