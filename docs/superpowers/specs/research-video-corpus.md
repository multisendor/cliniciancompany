## 6. Trust signals ranked by frequency in brand-review videos

Ranked from most-asked to least:

1. **Fibrinolytic units (FU) on label, not just mg** — every single brand-review video leads with this. ConsumerLab: 3 of 8 popular brands failed FU testing. **Highest priority signal.**
2. **3rd-party / independent lab tested for potency** — Natural Informant w/ Danny Curtin video literally titled around this. Buyers want a Certificate of Analysis (CoA).
3. **Clinical-grade dose (≥6,000 FU; gold standard 10,800 FU/day)** — repeated by every educated commenter. 4,000 FU per cap is positioned by buyers as *"now I can hit clinical dose with 2-3 caps."*
4. **Non-GMO soy** — Berg's standing requirement; comments echo it constantly.
5. **Vegan capsules** — assumption among health-buyer base; mentioned often, rarely contested.
6. **K2 status disclosed (in or removed)** — buyers split: those wanting cardiovascular protection want K2 retained; those on warfarin or already supplementing K2 want it removed. **PDP must explicitly state which.**
7. **Fermented from real Bacillus subtilis natto, not synthetic** — *"Is it L-ascorbic acid? No? So why should I take it?"* logic applied here.
8. **Free of fillers / sorbitol / additives** — *"I see it has sorbitol (ugg)"* @paulseminara2483.
9. **Made in USA / GMP-certified facility** — assumed but appreciated when stated.
10. **Doctor-formulated / cardiologist-endorsed** — when present, cited approvingly; missing is fine if other signals strong.
11. **Money-back guarantee / batch transparency** — emerging signal in 2025-2026 reviews.

## Strategic implications for the PDP (bonus)

- **Lead with FU dosing transparency** — this is the #1 trust gap in the category.
- **Pre-empt the destabilize-plaque fear** with Dr. Ham's "renovation" reframe.
- **Quote the Chen 2022 study by name** ("36% plaque reduction in 1,062 people at 10,800 FU/day — *Frontiers in Cardiovascular Medicine*") above the fold.
- **Address blood-thinner contraindication explicitly** — this question appears in every comment thread.
- **Decide and disclose K2 status** — both "with K2" and "K2 removed" have constituencies; pick one and own it.
- **Borrow the long-covid voice-of-customer**, not generic "feel better" copy. The audience is sophisticated, traumatized, and anti-marketing-speak. They respond to specifics: years sick, doctors visited, labs normal, lost careers.
- **Tone: hopeful but never cure-claim.** Norwitz's "promising, not definitive" register converts skeptics; "miracle cure" repels them.
- **Founder/origin story**: tell Dr. Sumi's 1980 Petri-dish discovery moment. The corpus knows the story but not the name. Naming him fills a void.
